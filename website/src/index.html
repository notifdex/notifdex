Built for triage, memory, and follow-through

Ephemeral banners disappear as soon as you swipe them away. Notifdex turns the alerts macOS exposes into an indexed inbox and relationship notebook you own on disk.

Inbox

Search, filter, flag, and silence noise

Filter by All, Unread, Read, Flagged, or Saved. Group by app, jump back with Open in app, and hide recurring subjects with reversible rules and Undo.

People & Workspaces

One timeline across every messaging app

Explicitly link Gmail, Messages, WhatsApp, X, Slack, Discord, or LinkedIn alerts to a person. Organize people across multiple workspaces with versioned relationship templates — never auto-merged by display name alone.

Today & Notes

Follow-ups and shared context in one place

Attach due dates to alerts, snooze or complete tasks in Today, and keep structured foldered Notes alongside shared People Notes that update everywhere you edit them.

Recovery & Reliability

Durable SQLite and honest coverage gaps

Every observation is committed transactionally with provenance and banner revisions. When the Mac sleeps or permissions pause, Notifdex logs the gap and lets you scan retained Notification Center stacks.

Local-first by architecture, not just policy

Notifdex operates solely on notification text macOS exposes locally via Accessibility. It never asks for your social or email passwords, and your database stays on your Mac.

Single local SQLite database

Captured alerts, People profiles, notes, and follow-ups live under ~/Library/Application Support/Notifdex with private file permissions and an exclusive collector lock.

No cloud accounts required

App toggles match observed macOS notification labels (X, Messages, Gmail, WhatsApp, Slack, Discord, LinkedIn, or custom labels) without connecting to third-party APIs.

Static public website

notifdex.com is a static Cloudflare Pages site with zero tracking cookies, zero telemetry, and zero connection to your Mac or database. Read our Privacy details.

Build and run Notifdex on macOS

Notifdex is currently distributed from source while signed public beta packaging (#20 and #22) is in progress. Building requires Apple's Command Line Tools on macOS.

Build & install locally from source 
git clone https://github.com/qzyu999/notifdex.git
cd notifdex
bash tools/build-app.sh
mkdir -p "$HOME/Applications"
ditto dist/Notifdex.app "$HOME/Applications/Notifdex.app"
open "$HOME/Applications/Notifdex.app"

First-run checklist

  1. Click the Notifdex menu bar icon and open App Center.
  2. Grant Notifdex Accessibility permission in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
  3. Enable the apps you want to index under Apps, then send a test notification.
