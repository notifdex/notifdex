Your iPhone notifications, kept quietly on your Mac.

A message arrives on your phone, appears for a second, and is gone. Notifdex lives in your Mac's menu bar, indexes the notifications macOS receives from your iPhone and desktop, and keeps them in a single file on your disk.

A quiet index for fleeting alerts.

Most important pings start on your iPhone. With iPhone notifications forwarded to your Mac, Notifdex records what macOS shows—nothing more, nothing hidden.

01

iPhone & Mac, one inbox

Search alerts across Messages, WhatsApp, Gmail, X, Slack, Discord, and LinkedIn. Flag what needs a reply, or hide recurring noise with a rule you can undo.

02

People, not just threads

Link alerts from different apps to one person, across as many workspaces as you keep. Notifdex never merges people automatically by display name.

03

Follow-ups and notes

Attach a due date to any notification and work through it in Today. Notes on a person's card stay in sync with your personal notebook.

04

Local, and honest about gaps

Everything lives in a single SQLite file on your Mac. No sign-in, no sync server. When your Mac sleeps or macOS hides a preview, Notifdex logs the gap rather than guessing.

Build on macOS

Notifdex is built from source while signed public releases (#20, #22) are underway. Requires Apple Command Line Tools.

  1. Turn on Allow notifications from iPhone in macOS Settings if you want iPhone alerts indexed.
  2. Open App Center from the menu bar and grant Accessibility access.
  3. Choose the apps to watch under Apps.
