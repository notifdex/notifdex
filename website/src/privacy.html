Privacy

Your notifications, people, and notes stay on your Mac. This website is static, sets no cookies, and has no connection to your device.

The macOS app

  • Local storage: Captured alerts, people links, notes, and follow-ups are written to ~/Library/Application Support/Notifdex/capture.db with private file permissions. SQLite itself is unencrypted; macOS FileVault protects the disk at rest.
  • No account logins: Notifdex reads notification previews macOS exposes via Accessibility—including iPhone notifications forwarded to your Mac—for apps you enable. It never asks for your email or messaging credentials.
  • Honest gaps: Locked-screen redactions, Focus modes, and Mac sleep can leave gaps. Notifdex records coverage gaps instead of claiming complete capture.
  • Optional AI: Any future summarization or chat features (#5, #48) will be strictly opt-in while local indexing remains fully offline.

This website

  • Static files only: Served on Cloudflare Pages with no third-party scripts, fonts, or tracking cookies. Every notification shown in the preview is fictional.
  • Network transport: Cloudflare handles standard HTTPS edge delivery and abuse protection transiently.

Reporting issues

When filing a bug on GitHub Issues, share only sanitized diagnostics or synthetic test alerts—never personal messages or capture.db.
