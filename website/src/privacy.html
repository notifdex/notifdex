Privacy & Local Data

Notifdex is designed so your notification history, People profiles, and notes stay on your Mac — not on our servers.

Short version: The public website at notifdex.com is a static site with no user accounts, no tracking cookies, and no access to your Mac or notifications. The macOS app stores all captured alerts and notes locally in SQLite on your device.

1. The Notifdex macOS app

  • Local storage only: Captured notification previews, Accessibility metadata, People links, notes, and follow-ups are stored in ~/Library/Application Support/Notifdex/capture.db with private file permissions. Note that SQLite itself is unencrypted on disk; standard macOS FileVault disk encryption protects data at rest.
  • No account credentials: Notifdex reads notification previews macOS exposes via Accessibility for the apps you explicitly enable. It does not sign in to your email, messaging, or social accounts.
  • Best-effort capture and gaps: macOS may redact locked-screen previews or clear banners while your Mac is asleep. Notifdex records coverage gaps transparently rather than claiming complete delivery.
  • Future optional AI features: Any future AI summarization or chat capabilities (tracked in #5 and #48) will require explicit opt-in and keep local capture and storage usable offline.

2. The public website (notifdex.com)

  • Static hosting: Hosted on Cloudflare Pages. All examples shown in the interactive preview use fictional, synthetic sample data.
  • No cookies or third-party trackers: This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies and loads no third-party scripts or fonts.
  • Infrastructure logs: Standard CDN edge networking (Cloudflare) processes IP addresses and request metadata transiently to serve HTTPS traffic and mitigate abuse.

3. Reporting bugs without leaking personal data

When opening a support issue on GitHub Issues, please share only sanitized diagnostics (scan counts, app labels, and synthetic test messages) and never upload personal messages or your capture.db file.
