Privacy

Your notifications, people, and notes stay on your own devices by default. There is no Notifdex account, no Notifdex sync server, and no analytics or ad tracking—and this website sets no tracking cookies and never touches your device.

The macOS & iOS apps

  • Local-first storage: On macOS, everything you capture—alerts, people, notes, and follow-ups—lives in ~/Library/Application Support/Notifdex/capture.db with private file permissions (protected at rest by macOS FileVault). On iOS, captured notifications and notes stay in an app-private SQLite database protected by iOS Data Protection (CompleteUntilFirstUserAuthentication).
  • No account logins: On macOS, Notifdex reads the notification previews macOS already shows through Accessibility for the apps you turn on. On iOS, capture runs only through an explicit Apple Shortcuts Notification automation you configure yourself. Notifdex never asks for your email or messaging passwords.
  • Straight about what it misses: Locked-screen redactions, Focus modes, and Mac sleep can leave gaps. Notifdex records when it wasn’t watching rather than claiming it caught everything.
  • No tracking or third-party SDKs: Neither app includes analytics, crash telemetry SDKs, ad networks, or third-party trackers.
  • Optional AI later: Any future summarization or chat features (#5, #48) will be strictly opt-in. Local indexing and notes will always work offline.

Optional private iCloud sync

  • Off by default: Both the macOS and iOS apps work completely offline until you explicitly enable and pair iCloud Sync.
  • Your Apple iCloud Private Database only: When paired, Notifdex syncs your Inbox triage, flags, follow-ups, People, and Notes through Apple CloudKit’s Private Database (container iCloud.com.notifdex.shared) inside your own Apple Account. Notifdex does not operate a backend server and cannot see or access your iCloud data.
  • Hidden & muted alerts stay local: Notifications you hide or filter out on your Mac are excluded from iCloud sync and never leave your Mac.
  • One-tap revocation: You can revoke any paired Mac or iPhone at any time from Recovery → iOS & iCloud Sync on macOS or Setup → iCloud Sync on iOS.

This website

  • Static files only: Served on Cloudflare Pages with no third-party scripts, external fonts, or tracking cookies. Every message in the interactive preview is made up.
  • Standard web delivery: Cloudflare handles HTTPS delivery and basic abuse protection in transit.

Reporting issues

If you open a bug report on GitHub Issues, please share only sanitized diagnostics or synthetic test alerts—never personal messages or your capture.db file.
